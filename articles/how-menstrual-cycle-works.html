How the Menstrual Cycle Works

A simple educational overview of menstrual cycle phases and why timing can vary.

The menstrual cycle in simple terms

The menstrual cycle is a repeating process involving hormonal changes and the uterus. Cycle length is counted from the first day of one period to the first day of the next.

Why cycles vary

A cycle does not have to be exactly 28 days. Normal biological variation, illness, stress and life changes can affect timing.

What a calendar tool can do

A calculator can provide planning estimates from past cycle information, but it cannot confirm what is happening biologically in a particular cycle.
