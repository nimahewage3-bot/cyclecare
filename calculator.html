ABOUT THE CALCULATION

How the CycleCare period calculator works

The tool uses the first day of your latest period and your average cycle length to estimate the next period. A simple calendar model then estimates ovulation about 14 days before the predicted next period.

Next period

Estimated from your last period start date plus your average cycle length.

Ovulation

Estimated from the predicted next period using a simple calendar assumption.

Fertile window

Shown as an estimate around the predicted ovulation date, not a guarantee of fertility.
