FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is the next period estimated?

The calculator adds your average cycle length to the first day of your last period.

How is ovulation estimated?

For this simple model, ovulation is estimated as 14 days before the next predicted period.

Are these dates guaranteed?

No. They are estimates. Actual cycle timing can change.

Can I use this to prevent pregnancy?

No. CycleCare should not be used as a contraception method because fertile-window estimates can be inaccurate.

Does CycleCare require an account?

No. The basic calculator works without an account. Settings can be stored locally in your browser.

When should I seek medical advice?

Consider speaking with a qualified healthcare professional about severe pain, unusual bleeding, missed periods or significant changes in your usual cycle.
