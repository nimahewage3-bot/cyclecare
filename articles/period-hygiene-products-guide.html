Period Hygiene: Pads, Tampons, Cups & Period Underwear

There is no single “best” period product. Comfort, flow, access and personal preference all matter.

Updated: September 24, 2026 · Educational information only

Common options

Period products include disposable and reusable pads, tampons, menstrual cups, menstrual discs and period underwear. Different products suit different bodies and routines.

Pads

Pads are worn outside the body and come in different sizes and absorbencies. Change them regularly and follow the product instructions. Most people change pads every few hours, sooner when they are soaked or uncomfortable.

Tampons

Tampons are inserted into the vagina. Use the lowest absorbency that manages your flow and follow the package instructions. The U.S. Office on Women’s Health advises not wearing a tampon for more than 8 hours.

Menstrual cups and discs

Reusable products collect menstrual blood rather than absorb it. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions for insertion, removal, cleaning and how long the product can stay in place.

Period underwear

Period underwear can absorb menstrual fluid and may be used alone or with another product depending on flow. Check the care instructions so the fabric is cleaned thoroughly.

Hygiene matters more than the brand

Wash your hands before handling internal products, change products regularly, clean reusable products as directed and avoid fragranced products that can irritate the vulva.
