What Is Ovulation?

An educational explanation of ovulation and the limits of calendar-based predictions.

What ovulation means

Ovulation is the release of an egg from an ovary. Its timing can vary between people and from one cycle to another.

Calendar estimates

A simple calculator may estimate ovulation by counting backward from an estimated next period. That assumption does not confirm actual ovulation.

Why the distinction matters

A calendar prediction should not be treated as a guaranteed fertile or infertile day. For reproductive or medical planning, use appropriate professional guidance.
