How to Calculate Your Next Period

Learn the simple calendar method used by CycleCare to estimate a future period.

The basic formula

Start with the first day of the most recent period and add the average cycle length. A 28-day average therefore gives a simple estimate about 28 days after the previous start date.

Why the result can be different

The method assumes the upcoming cycle will be similar to the average. Real cycles can be shorter or longer, so an estimate may not match the actual date.

Tracking over time

Recording actual period start dates over several months can help reveal a person's usual pattern. Significant changes or concerning symptoms should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
