About CycleCare

CycleCare is a free educational tool for simple calendar-based menstrual cycle estimates.

Our approach

CycleCare uses the cycle information a visitor enters to calculate estimated dates. Because cycle timing can vary, every result is presented as an estimate.

The basic tools are designed to work without an account.

What it is not

CycleCare is not a medical service or diagnostic tool. It cannot confirm ovulation, fertility or pregnancy and should not be used as a contraception method.
