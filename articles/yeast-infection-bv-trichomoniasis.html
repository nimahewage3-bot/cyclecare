VAGINAL HEALTH

Yeast Infection, BV or Something Else? Understanding Vaginal Symptoms

Itching, burning and discharge can have several causes, so treating yourself blindly is not always the best approach.

Updated: September 24, 2026 · Educational information only

Vaginitis has many causes

Vaginitis means inflammation of the vagina and can be caused by yeast overgrowth, bacterial vaginosis (BV), trichomoniasis, hormonal changes, irritation or other conditions.

Yeast infection pattern

A vaginal yeast infection may cause itching and burning of the vulva, redness or swelling, and sometimes thicker white discharge. These symptoms can overlap with other problems.

Bacterial vaginosis pattern

BV is linked to an imbalance in vaginal bacteria. A common symptom is increased discharge with a strong fish-like odor. BV can occur whether or not a person has had vaginal sex.

Why guessing can backfire

Symptoms are not specific enough to diagnose every case at home. A person who assumes “yeast infection” may actually have BV, an STI, a skin condition or another cause. In those situations, the wrong over-the-counter treatment may delay the correct diagnosis.

When to get checked

Seek professional advice for a first episode of significant vaginal symptoms, symptoms that keep returning, symptoms that do not improve after treatment, pelvic pain, fever, sores, unusual bleeding or a possible STI exposure.

Protect the natural balance

Gentle external hygiene, avoiding douching and scented products, and using protection during sex can reduce some forms of irritation or infection risk, but no hygiene routine prevents every vaginal infection.
